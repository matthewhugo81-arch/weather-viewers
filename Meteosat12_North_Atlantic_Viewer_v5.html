Viewer settings

The viewer constructs individual Meteosat-12 archive-image URLs at 10-minute intervals and can work back through 24 hours. Missing daylight/night-only frames are simply skipped. Refreshes re-check previously missing frames, and animation begins only after the selected archive window has been fully scanned.
